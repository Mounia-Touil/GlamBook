Skip the phone calls. Just browse, tap, and book your beauty appointment online with ease.

Explore services like hair, nails, facials, lashes and more. Pick a date and time that fits your schedule, and confirm in one click.

Manage all your bookings in one place. View clients, schedules, and upcoming appointments effortlessly.

Salon owners can add custom services with descriptions, prices, and timing. Attract more clients by showcasing what you offer.

Set your availability and let clients choose from open slots — no overbooking, no hassle.

Local Discovery

Clients can discover salons near them with search filters by service, time, and location.
